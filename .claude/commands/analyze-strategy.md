# Analyze Trading Strategy

Analyze and explain a specific trading strategy from the trading bot.

Available strategies to analyze:
- **OmegaMode**: 3-layer meta-cognitive framework (Layer 1: Pattern Recognition, Layer 2: Risk Analysis, Layer 3: Meta-Cognitive)
- **AICouncil**: Multi-AI ensemble with consensus voting (DeepSeek, OpenAI, Claude)
- **TechnicalIndicators**: Classic indicators (RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, ATR)
- **AIStrategyEngine**: Gradient Boosting ML classifier

Please read trading_bot.py and analyze the requested strategy component, explaining:
1. How the strategy works
2. Key parameters and thresholds
3. Risk management integration
4. Potential improvements
